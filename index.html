Real tokenizers (GPT, Llama) learn 32,000 to 200,000 merges from terabytes of text. Words the corpus rarely saw, like Darija written in Latin script, split into more tokens and cost more.

Scaled dot-product attention

Click any word to make it the query. Its attention over the sentence is computed with the real formula on small hand-made vectors, so you can follow every number. Then switch the last word and watch “it” change its mind.

softmax( q·kᵢ / √d ) , d = 6

Dimensions: noun, animate, place, function word, verb, pronoun. Real models learn Q, K and V matrices with hundreds of dimensions; these are hand-picked to show the mechanics.

Next-token sampling and temperature

A language model outputs a score (logit) for every token. Softmax with temperature T turns scores into probabilities: p ∝ exp(logit / T). Move T and sample to see how “creative” the model becomes.

Gold column = how often each token came out in your 100 samples. Logits are illustrative values for teaching, not taken from a specific model.
